Privacy policy

Updated September 21, 2026

Privacy policy Data deletion instructions Service terms

Mars B Digital operates ChatbotX so authorized businesses can receive and respond to messages sent to their connected Facebook Pages. Each connected business controls its Page, reply instructions and authorized workspace users. Mars B Digital operates the service that processes those conversations. This policy explains the messaging information handled by this service. For questions or requests about that information, email Marsbdigital@gmail.com.

Information we process

When an authorized administrator connects a Facebook Page, we process the Page identity and the connection information needed to operate messaging. When someone messages the Page, we may process their Page-scoped sender identifier, profile details supplied by Meta, message text, attachments, conversation and delivery timestamps, and replies. Information you choose to include in a message may also be stored in the conversation. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details.

How the information is used

We use this information to display and route conversations, provide human or automated replies, manage the connected Page, protect the service, and investigate delivery or deletion failures. Access within the app is controlled through account and workspace permissions.

When an AI assistant is enabled, the assistant can receive the current message and relevant prior conversation context. The reviewer workspace uses OpenAI to generate its test replies. The AI provider processes the information sent to it under its own applicable terms. Automated replies may be incorrect; contact the Page or Mars B Digital when a human response is needed.

Services involved

Meta carries messages to and from the Facebook Page. The service's hosting, database and file-storage systems process information needed to operate ChatbotX. OpenAI processes the conversation information sent to the enabled AI assistant. This policy covers information under Mars B Digital's control; Meta and other connected businesses may hold their own copies under their policies.

Retention and deletion

Message and attachment data remains in the service until it is removed through the applicable account or deletion-request process. Verified deletion requests are processed against the relevant contact and conversation records. A successful cleanup retains a minimal operational receipt for 30 days, containing a random receipt identifier, workspace identifier, completion time, number of attempts and implementation version. It does not retain the sender identifier, contact or conversation identifiers, message content or file paths. Failed cleanup work retains the information needed to finish the request and is reviewed by the operator.

Backup copies, operational logs, human request correspondence and provider-held information are separate from active conversation storage. We will explain the applicable handling and any required retention when responding to a request. Removing a Facebook connection alone does not necessarily delete messages already received by this service.

Requests and contact

Email Marsbdigital@gmail.com to request access, correction, export or deletion of information under our control. Identify the Facebook Page and the conversation or account concerned. Do not send a password or identity document in your first message. We may ask for limited information needed to verify your identity or authority before acting.

Questions? Contact Mars B Digital.
