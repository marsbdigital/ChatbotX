Updated September 21, 2026

Privacy policy Data deletion instructions Service terms

If you messaged a Facebook Page connected to Mars B Digital ChatbotX, or administer a connected Page, email Marsbdigital@gmail.com with the subject Meta data deletion request.

Include the Page name or link and enough information to identify the relevant conversation or account. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or identity documents in the initial request.

We will verify the requester's identity or authority, identify the data under our control, and process the applicable contact, conversation and attachment deletion. If a technical failure prevents completion, we retain the deletion job for investigation and retry; we do not treat a failed job as completed. We will explain any retained backup, log, legal or provider-held information relevant to your request.

Disconnecting the app from Facebook stops the relevant connection but does not by itself erase previously received conversation data. Meta's own information and copies held by other parties must be managed through those parties' processes.

Questions? Contact Mars B Digital.
